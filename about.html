About NovaX Edu

Empowering students across Sri Lanka with quality education, expert teachers, and innovative learning experiences since 2020.

Our Mission

To democratize education in Sri Lanka by providing accessible, high-quality learning resources to every student, regardless of their location or economic background.

We believe that quality education should not be a privilege but a right. Through our platform, we connect students with the best teachers across the country, offering both free and premium content to ensure no student is left behind.
