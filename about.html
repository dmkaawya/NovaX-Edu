How NovaX Edu began

It started in 2023 when a small group of Korean language enthusiasts — students, teachers, and developers — came together with a simple frustration: why is learning Korean so fragmented?

Video lessons on one platform. Flashcards on another. Quizzes somewhere else. No unified experience, no progress tracking, no community. We decided to change that.

NovaX Edu was born from late-night coding sessions, endless cups of coffee, and a shared passion for making Korean accessible to everyone — whether you're a complete beginner or preparing for TOPIK.

Today, we're a growing community of 2,400+ learners across 30+ countries, and we're just getting started. 한국어를 배우자 — let's learn Korean together.

Our Values

What drives us forward

These core principles shape every feature, every lesson, and every decision we make.

Accessibility First
Korean education should be available to everyone, everywhere — regardless of budget, location, or background. That's why our core content is always free.
Quality Over Quantity
Every lesson is carefully crafted, reviewed, and tested. We'd rather have 100 outstanding lessons than 1,000 mediocre ones.
Continuous Growth
We ship improvements weekly. New lessons, better features, bug fixes — our platform evolves based on real learner feedback.
Community Driven
Learners help each other. Our discussion forum, study groups, and peer support make learning Korean a social experience, not a lonely one.
Science-Backed
Spaced repetition, active recall, interleaving — our tools are built on proven learning science, not guesswork.
Privacy & Trust
Your data is yours. No ads, no selling data, no tracking. We build a platform you can trust with your learning journey.
Our Journey

Milestones along the way

From a small idea to a growing movement — here's how we got here.

January 2023
The Idea Was Born
A group of 5 Korean learners and 2 native teachers started brainstorming the ideal Korean learning platform over a weekend hackathon.
June 2023
First 50 Lessons Launched
Video lessons, PDF notes, and quizzes for Hangul basics and elementary Korean went live. Our first 200 users joined in the first month.
December 2025
Flashcards & Study Planner
We introduced spaced repetition flashcards and a study planner feature. User engagement increased by 340%.
April 2025
Community Hits 1,000
Our discussion forum became the heart of the platform. Learners were helping each other 24/7 across different time zones.
October 2025
TOPIK Prep Courses
Launched comprehensive TOPIK I & II preparation tracks with mock exams. Several students reported passing TOPIK Level 4+.
2026 — Now
2,400+ Learners Strong
30+ countries, 500+ lessons, and a mission that's bigger than ever. We're building the future of Korean language education.
